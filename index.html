COMPUTER ARCHITECTURE & MEMORY SYSTEMS

Pengchen Zong.

Understanding what comes next.
Making systems ready for it.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the School of Artificial Intelligence at Xi’an Jiaotong University, co-advised by Prof. Pengju Ren and Prof. Tian Xia. I am affiliated with the State Key Laboratory of Human-Machine Hybrid Augmented Intelligence and the Institute of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics.

My research spans computer architecture, hardware–software co-design, and memory systems, with a particular focus on prefetching for irregular workloads. I am also exploring how execution plans and runtime feedback can improve the efficiency of AI agents on CPU–GPU systems.

Before my Ph.D., I worked as a digital IC design engineer at HiSilicon, focusing on SoC debugging, observability, and integration.

Research directions

Program structure can reveal future work before execution reaches it. My research asks how systems can turn that information into useful lookahead.

RESEARCH FOUNDATION
Program structure→Lookahead→Prefetch

Anticipating irregular memory accesses

I study how loop structure, program order, and data movement can help prefetchers look ahead across indirect accesses and linked data structures.

PrefetchingMemory systemsMicroarchitecture
EMERGING INTEREST
Plan⇄Runtime feedback⇄CPU / GPU

Making agent execution hardware-aware

Explicit agent plans may reveal upcoming data and execution-state needs. I am exploring how runtime feedback can guide preparation across actions while accounting for uncertain branches, bandwidth, memory capacity, and compute.
